# Frequently asked questions

## How much notice do custom cakes need?

Custom cakes need 3 days notice.

Please order a custom cake by e-mail or at the counter.

## How can I pay?

You can pay by card or cash.

We do not accept cheques.

## Are dogs welcome?

Dogs are welcome on the terrace.

## Is there wifi in the cafe?

There is free wifi in the cafe.

## Where can I park?

You can park behind the shop where there are 12 parking spaces.

## Can a school group visit the bakery?

School group visits can be booked by e-mail, see the [contact page](contact.html).

## Is the bakery a franchise?

The bakery has no franchise and there is only one shop.
