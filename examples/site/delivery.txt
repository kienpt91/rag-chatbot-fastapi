Delivery

Delivery area
We deliver within 5 kilometres of the shop.
Addresses further than 5 kilometres from the shop are not delivered.

Delivery fee
The delivery fee is 5 dollars.
Delivery is free for orders above 40 dollars.
The minimum order for delivery is 15 dollars.

Delivery days and times
Orders placed before 10:00 are delivered the same day on weekdays.
There is no delivery on Sundays.
To place an order, e-mail us using the address on the contact page.
