# Menu and prices

## Breads

A sourdough loaf costs 6.50 dollars.

A rye loaf costs 7.00 dollars.

A baguette costs 3.00 dollars.

## Pastries

A croissant costs 3.50 dollars.

A cinnamon roll costs 4.00 dollars.

## Drinks

A cup of filter coffee costs 2.50 dollars.

A cup of hot chocolate costs 3.50 dollars.

## Vegan and gluten-free options

The bakery offers vegan bread and vegan pastries every day.

The gluten-free loaf is baked on Fridays only.

You can order the gluten-free loaf in advance, see the [delivery page](delivery.txt) for ordering.

## Allergens

Nuts are handled in the same kitchen as all our breads and pastries.

Please tell the staff about any allergy before you order.
