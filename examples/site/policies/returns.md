# Returns and refunds

## Pre-orders

Uncollected pre-orders are kept until 17:00 the next day.

## Refunds

Refunds for damaged goods are given within 24 hours with a photo.

There are no refunds on custom cakes after baking has started.

Questions about ordering cakes are answered in the [FAQ](../faq.md).

## Gift cards

Gift cards are valid for 12 months.
