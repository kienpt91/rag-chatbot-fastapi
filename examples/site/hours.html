Opening hours

Regular hours

The bakery opens at 7:00 in the morning and closes at 18:00 on weekdays, from Monday to Friday.

On Saturday the bakery opens at 8:00 and closes at 14:00, so the Saturday opening hours are 8:00 to 14:00.

On Sunday the bakery is closed.

DayOpening hours
Monday to Friday7:00 to 18:00
Saturday8:00 to 14:00
SundayClosed

Holidays

The bakery is closed on December 25 and on January 1.

On all other public holidays the bakery keeps its regular opening hours.

Cafe last orders

The last orders for the cafe are taken 30 minutes before closing time.

Bread and pastries can be bought at the counter until closing time.

To reach us outside these hours, see the contact page.
